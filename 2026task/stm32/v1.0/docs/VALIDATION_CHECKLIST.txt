   Issue: Integral accumulates 5x slower (100Hz vs 500Hz)
   Impact: May need to increase SPEED_KI by ~5x if steady-state error increases
   Current SPEED_KI: 50.0
   Suggested range if needed: 100-250
   Monitor: Steady-state velocity error during testing

⚠ 2. Acceleration Limiting
   Issue: Time step increased from 2ms → 10ms (5x)
   Impact: max_delta = MAX_ACCELERATION × dt increases 5x
   Example: MAX_ACCELERATION = 2.0 m/s²
            Old: 2.0 × 0.002 = 0.004 m/s per update
            New: 2.0 × 0.010 = 0.020 m/s per update
   Monitor: Verify acceleration limiting still prevents wheel slip

⚠ 3. Phase Lag
   Issue: Lower control frequency increases phase lag
   Impact: At 100Hz, phase lag ≈ 5ms (half period)
   Monitor: Track following accuracy, especially in curves

⚠ 4. Encoder Count Accumulation
   Issue: Encoder counts accumulate for 2ms (500Hz) vs get consumed every 10ms (100Hz)
   Impact: None - encoder still samples at 500Hz, velocity estimate remains accurate
   Monitor: Verify velocity feedback is smooth
